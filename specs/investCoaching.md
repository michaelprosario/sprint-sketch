**INVEST** is a coaching mnemonic that helps teams evaluate whether a user story is well‑formed and ready for a sprint. Each letter represents a quality of a strong Agile story.

## INVEST — Concise Description
- **Independent** — The story stands on its own and can be delivered without relying on another story.
- **Negotiable** — It isn’t a contract; details can evolve through conversation between the team and product owner.
- **Valuable** — It delivers clear value to the user or customer, not just internal technical work.
- **Estimable** — The team can size it because scope, complexity, and constraints are reasonably understood.
- **Small** — It fits within a sprint and can be completed without spanning multiple iterations.
- **Testable** — There are clear acceptance criteria or conditions of satisfaction that allow verification.
